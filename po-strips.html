The job

Cut and build the strips that go under the three plant room frames: the small window (932) at the plant end, the big window (1552) at the office end and the bifold door (1263) in the front wall.

Same rule as the Lodge: everything finishes flush with the outside face of the CLT. The plant room frames sit 7.5 further out than the Lodge's, so anything that sits up inside the floor slot comes 7.5 narrower.

Before you cut

  • Small window: the floor slot is 13 short, like the wall. When Wall 7 is ripped and moved 13 towards the back wall, also cut 13 more off the north end of the slot in the floor (the end nearest the back wall), right through, 42.5 in from the gable face. Then the slot should measure 938 along its inner edge and 978 at the face. The frame sits down in that slot. Do it before the gable goes up if you can: it's a cut right through the 60 floor.
  • Measure a thermal-break board first. You had them at 1200 × 1025 (the label says 1220 × 1005). Everything below fits a 1200 length.
  • Cut every ply strip across the sheet, 1220 long, so the grain runs across the strip. Never rip lengthways.
  • Saw cut: 3 on ply, 2 on thermal break.
  • Thermal break: from the piece of board 2 you kept back (about 400 wide). The five strips use about 240 of it.
  • Ply: 18 mm marine ply (sheet 2). If sheet 2 is now only 1098 wide (a 120 came off it for the bridge roof), see the note under the cut list.
  • Birch: the 405 × 560 piece of 25 mm birch, if it's still about.

  1. Ply flap, one layer. Glue it (PU, full area) and screw it up into the underside of the floor. Push it back tight against the joist ends: its front edge then lands flush with the outside face of the CLT. Centre it on the opening so it runs 10 past each end of the frame. Screws no longer than 70: the floor is only 60 thick.
  2. Thermal break on the flap, filling the floor slot from its inner edge out to the outside face: 42.5 wide, flush. Cut it to the same length as the frame, no longer: the inner edge of the slot is only 6 longer than the frame.
  3. Check: the frame sits on the thermal break with its bottom 35 below the floor surface (25 up from the underside of the CLT). It stands 30 past the outside face (22.5 on the Lodge), out over the cill.

Plant room vs the Lodge

  • The walls are 60 thick, not 100. The frames sit 40 into the wall, not 47.5, and the floor slot is 7.5 shallower to match (42.5 at the windows, 40 at the bifold).
  • The outside face stays the line. Everything finishes flush with it, as on the Lodge. So what's under the floor keeps its width, and what's in the slot loses 7.5.
  • Windows: flap 100 (joist ends to the outside face, the same on both buildings) and thermal break 42.5 (the Lodge's 50 filled a 50 slot). The flap length is the frame plus 20.
  • The joists are 100 in from the edge on both buildings.
