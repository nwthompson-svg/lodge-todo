1. Lodge inside backers

2×2 treated. These go on the inside face of the insulation at the bottom of the wall (under the floor), opposite the low battens, so the screws clamp the insulation between them. They hold the insulation up under the doors.

Before you cut

  • Every length: cut 50 off one end first (the bad end), then measure from that cut.
  • Treat every cut end.
  • Measure the real thickness of the 2×2 before cutting the gable pieces.
  • Measure the gap from the inside face of the insulation to the joist edge in a few places before cutting noggins.

2. Plant room battens

Same system as the Lodge. Four walls: the back (metal sheet), the front (bifold door), the plant end (small window) and the office end (big window).

How to cut the 4×1

  • Cut 50 off one end of each 4.8 m board, then measure from that cut.
  • Cross-cut first, rip second. Cut the board into the pieces on its line below.
  • Pieces marked RIP get ripped in half with the chamfer, like the Lodge, so each one makes two battens.
  • "then cut one down to …" means after the rip, chop one of the pair to the second length.
  • Pieces marked DON'T RIP are the over/under battens: full 95 width, chamfered top.
  • Label both edges on the back before ripping. Treat every cut end.
