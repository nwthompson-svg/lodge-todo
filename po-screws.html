The job

Drill and screw the three plant room frames into the CLT: the small window at the plant end, the big window at the office end and the bifold door in the front wall.

It's the same method and the same positions as the Lodge. There's one change: the bifold head takes 60s, not 70s.

Method: same on every frame

  • Positions: first screw 150 from each corner, the rest evenly spaced, never more than 600 apart. The numbers are measured from the corner: jambs up from the bottom, head and base from the left, looking from inside.
  • Access hole: 9.5 step drill through the face of the frame. The pan head goes in and bears on the inner wall of the frame. A black cap covers the hole later. Use the long T25 bit.
  • 5.0 clearance through the aluminium and the plastic insert in the middle of the frame (the frame's own thermal break). The thread must not bite either. No countersinking anywhere.
  • Pilots: none into the CLT (wax the screw, drive on the clutch). 3.0 long-series into ply only, which means the base screws. Set a depth stop on the 5.0 at the frame plus the 25 thermal-break strip under it.
  • Wax every thread. Snug, never cranked. The packers set the height; the screws only hold it.
  • Seal any hole that goes through the frame's plastic insert. Keep 20 off any cut edge of the CLT.

Plant room vs the Lodge

  • Bifold head: 4.5 × 60, not 70. The plant room roof is only 60 thick (the Lodge roof is 100). A 70 can come out through the top of the roof.
  • Window heads stay 60s. They stay inside the roof.
  • Jambs and bases are the same as the Lodge. But the frames sit 40 into the wall here (47.5 on the Lodge), so every jamb and head screw is 7.5 nearer the outside face of the CLT. On the first hole of each edge, check it's at least 20 in from the outside face.
  • Small window: rip Wall 7 and cut the floor slot 13 longer before the walls go up (see the strips page). Otherwise the 932 won't go in.
