The job

Six timber blocks go into the plant room roof, on the foil, before the insulation. Later the solar panel frame screws down through the EPDM into them. The insulation is too soft to take a screw, so the blocks are the only solid places the frame can fix to.

Before you start

  • Wait for Neil's go before fitting any block. The positions and blocks are settled now; he'll tell you when.
  • Measure from the bare CLT edges of the roof: the plant end (small-window end) and the front (bifold side). Not from any insulation, battens or cladding.
  • Check the roof: 8400 long and 3396 front to back. If either is out by more than 10, stop and message Neil.
  • The long side of every block runs front to back.
  • Each block is as tall as the insulation at that spot. The boards are tapered, so cut each block against the board that goes there. Top flush, within 2.
  • The VGS go 50 into the CLT, never more. The roof is only 60 thick and the underside is the ceiling. A tip coming through shows inside.
  • Clear, straight-grained timber only: no knots or cracks within a hand's width of a screw. The blocks are narrow and a 9 mm screw can split them.
  • Cut the VGS tops off before any insulation is on the roof. Grinder sparks hole the foil and the insulation burns.
  • Blocks 5 and 6 (office end): Neil may put a second roof beam along that line instead, like the one already in. If he does, these two aren't needed.
  • Photo and measure every block and send them to Neil before the EPDM goes on. Once it's covered, nobody can find them.

3. Mark out
  1. On the foil, mark a line 780 back from the front edge. Measure at both ends and join the marks with a straight batten.
  2. Mark a second line 2170 back from the front edge the same way.
  3. Along both lines, mark 1783, 4200 and 6617 from the plant-end edge.
  4. Check from the other end: the marks should read 1783, 4200 and 6617 from the office-end edge too. If they're out by more than 10, stop and message Neil.
  5. Each cross is the centre of a block. Set the block on it, long side front to back.
  6. The 57 blocks go on the front line (780) and the 47 blocks on the back line (2170).
4. Fit
  1. Make six blocks, 350 long. Front row (blocks 1, 3, 5): from the 57 timber. Back row (2, 4, 6): from 4x2 on edge (47). Clear, straight grain.
  2. Height: the same as the insulation board that sits there. The top slopes about 6 down towards the back over the 350. Front blocks come out about 96–101, back blocks about 73–78. Cut against the actual board.
  3. Mark the screws on the middle line of the top (section 5): VGS at 70 from each end, plus one in the middle (175) on blocks 1, 2, 5 and 6. HBS at 100 from each end.
  4. Drill on the bench, right through the block: 5 mm at each VGS mark, 6.5 mm at each HBS mark. Counterbore the HBS holes with a 16 mm bit to the depth in the table.
  5. Bed each block on the foil on a bead of the insulation glue (the PU foam), on its marks.
  6. HBS 6 × 90 first, two per block. They pull the block down tight. About 20 goes into the CLT.
  7. Then the VGS 9 × 160. Clamp across the block at the screw with a sash clamp, then drive until the amount in the table is left sticking up. That puts it 50 into the CLT. Go slowly at the end. If anyone's inside, ask them to watch the ceiling under the block.
  8. Check every block for cracks. If one has split, make a new one.
  9. Cut the VGS off flush with the grinder, before any insulation is on the roof. Shield the foil from the sparks. File the cut ends smooth so they can't cut the EPDM.
  10. Photo each block from above with a tape showing both distances. Send them to Neil.
  11. Fit the insulation round tight and foam any gap. Check the top is flush with a straight edge across the block and the boards either side.
  12. The EPDM goes over the top as normal. Don't cut it at the blocks.
