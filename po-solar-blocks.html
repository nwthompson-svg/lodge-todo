The job

Six timber blocks go into the plant room roof, on the foil, before the insulation. Later the solar panel frame screws down through the EPDM into them. The insulation is too soft to take a screw, so the blocks are the only solid places the frame can fix to.

Before you start

  • Measure from the bare CLT edges of the roof: the plant end (small-window end) and the front (bifold side). Not from any insulation, battens or cladding.
  • Check the roof: 8400 long and 3396 front to back. If either is out by more than 10, stop and message Neil.
  • The long side of every block runs front to back.
  • Each block is as tall as the insulation at that spot. The boards are tapered, so cut each block against the board that goes there. Top flush, within 2.
  • Screws: no more than 50 into the CLT. The roof is only 60 thick and the underside is the ceiling. A tip coming through shows inside.
  • Blocks 5 and 6 (office end): wait for Neil's OK. He may put a second roof beam along that line instead, like the one at 5850.
  • Photo and measure every block and send them to Neil before the EPDM goes on. Once it's covered, nobody can find them.

3. Mark out
  1. On the foil, mark a line 780 back from the front edge. Measure at both ends and join the marks with a straight batten.
  2. Mark a second line 2170 back from the front edge the same way.
  3. Along both lines, mark 1783, 4200 and 6617 from the plant-end edge.
  4. Check from the other end: the marks should read 1783, 4200 and 6617 from the office-end edge too. If they're out by more than 10, stop and message Neil.
  5. Each cross is the centre of a block. Set the block on it, long side front to back.
4. Fit
  1. Make six blocks, 150 × 350, from treated timber. Two 6×2 offcuts glued and screwed together give 94, then cut down to height.
  2. Height: the same as the insulation board that sits there. If the board is thicker than your block, add a packer, glued and screwed.
  3. Bed each block on the foil on a bead of the insulation glue (the PU foam), on its marks.
  4. Six screws per block, 6 mm, in the pattern in section 5. Screw length = block height + 40 to 50 (table below). If you only have longer ones, counterbore.
  5. Fit the insulation round tight and foam any gap.
  6. Check the top is flush with a straight edge across the block and the boards either side.
  7. Photo each block from above with a tape showing both distances. Send them to Neil.
  8. The EPDM goes over the top as normal. Don't cut it at the blocks.
