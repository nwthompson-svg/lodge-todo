The job

Cut the membrane and the mouse mesh for the underside of the plant room floor. Every piece is the same length and runs across the floor, back to front. The pieces sit side by side along the 8.4 m with an even overlap.

3766every piece
6membrane, 46 overlap

Before you cut

  • Measure the joists. Outside of the back joist to outside of the front joist should be 3196 (measure away from the doubled joist at the bifold). End of the joists to end of the joists should be 8200. The joists sit 100 in from the CLT edge all round. If either is out by more than 10, stop and message Neil.
  • Joists not on yet? Measure the CLT instead: 3396 across, 8400 long. Cut anyway; the cut length doesn't change.
  • Check the mesh: 900 wide, 30 m on the label, and 2 rolls. If not, stop and message Neil: the layout changes.
  • Check the Traspir roll has at least 23 m left (6 pieces = 22.6 m).
  • A 30 m mesh roll gives 7 pieces, not 8 (8 would need 30.1 m). So 11 pieces = 7 off one roll + 4 off the next.
  • Label every piece as you cut it with a marker in one corner (M1 to M6, R1 to R11), and write BACK on the end that goes to the back.
  • Membrane goes on the same way up as the Lodge floor.

How to read the positions

All positions are measured along the edge joist from the end of the joists at the plant end (the small window end).

  • Mark the END of each piece (the green numbers) on the bare joists. That mark stays visible until that piece goes on. Start marks would be hidden under the piece before.
  • Mark on both the back and front joists, so each piece goes on square.
  • First piece (M1 / R1): hangs 285 past the joist end, far edge on its end mark.
  • Each next piece: far edge on its own end mark. It overlaps back over the last piece by itself.
  • Last piece (M6 / R11): finishes 285 past the joist end at the office end. Check it does.
  • Across the floor, every piece hangs 285 past the back joist and 285 past the front joist.
  • The layout works the same from either end and either way up, so it doesn't matter if the cassette is upside down.

4. The 285 at every edge

From the edge joist: 100 across to the CLT edge, 60 under the wood fibre, then 125 up its face (100 plus 25 spare). Same at the back, front and both ends.

Don't trim it. The wood fibre isn't on yet: leave it loose (roll it up and tack it out of the way if it's in the way) until it is.

5. Laying (when Neil says go)

Same as the Lodge floor

  1. Loft roll in between the joists first.
  2. Membrane: start with M1 at the plant end, far edge on its end marks, hanging 285 past the back joist.
  3. Staple to the bottom of every joist, about 150 apart.
  4. Each next piece overlaps the last by 46. Staple through both sheets at every joist, then tape the overlap (FLEXI) the full length.
  5. Mesh over the top the same way: R1 at the plant end, far edges on the mesh end marks.
  6. Wire-tie every mesh overlap closed, no more than 300 apart.
  7. Leave the 285 loose at all four edges (section 4).
